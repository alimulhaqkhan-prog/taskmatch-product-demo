Task BM · continuity layer

Chat history is not the same as task state.

Task BM is the product concept for remembering the evolving state of a service task: what was proposed, what both sides accepted, what is still pending, and what later changed or conflicted.

This is the core distinction from a simple chat + booking marketplace: important terms are intended to remain legible as the conversation evolves.

Research translated into product behavior

The model can change. The task understanding is meant to persist.

TaskMatch is not being designed to hand its entire intelligence to a general-purpose AI API. The current prototype already contains a separate continuity and intervention layer around the conversation; broader founder research informs where that layer can develop next.

Models can change. The task history should not.

The durable layer is the evolving record of real work: what each side proposed, understood, accepted, left unresolved, changed and finally confirmed. AI can help interpret and surface risk, but human-confirmed task state remains the authority.

Task BM + TM-Continuity-1.1 · implementedMaintains task terms, revisions, client/worker states, recent corrections and continuity checkpoints instead of treating chat history as the only source of truth.
Bilateral understanding gap M · implementedA proposal or acceptance by one side is not treated as mutual agreement. The engine separately tracks the client and worker view of the same term revision.
Ask → Verify → Update · implementedWhen ambiguity, unresolved commitments or conflicting understanding rises, the product logic is designed to clarify rather than silently guess or rewrite accepted Task BM.
TM-CIE-1.1 intervention heuristic · implementedA bounded engineering score combines ambiguity, contradiction, drift, unresolved commitments, safety and bilateral-understanding signals to decide whether to stay silent, suggest, clarify or escalate a safety review.
ZTM(t) = sigmoid(1.10A + 1.50C + 1.40D + 0.90U + 2.50S + 1.20M − 0.95)

A ambiguity · C contradiction · D drift from accepted Task BM · U unresolved required commitment · S safety/policy concern · M bilateral understanding gap.

Important boundary: ZTM is a TaskMatch product-engineering intervention score. It is not a claim that the app measures or validates the founder’s research latent variable Z.

General AI alone

  • Responds to the current prompt and supplied context.
  • Can translate, summarize and suggest language.
  • Its memory/state depends on what the application sends to it.
  • A model or provider can be replaced.

TaskMatch coordination layer

  • Maintains client/worker/task-specific continuity outside a single model call.
  • Tracks proposed, pending, accepted, changed and conflicting terms.
  • Detects drift against accepted Task BM and bilateral understanding gaps.
  • Keeps human confirmation authoritative before accepted task state changes.
